AI suggestions for fields and tags

On a document's page, an AI provider can propose its name, category, dates, amount, reference, counterparty, creator, language, description and tags from the text read from its PDFs. Nothing is sent until you press the button on that page and pick the files; nothing changes until you tick and apply a suggestion.

Every provider sends the text to its company: OpenAI and Anthropic over their API, Claude Code CLI and Kiro CLI through the CLI installed on this server. Keys and models are in Settings → AI Agent. When the chosen provider is not set up, nothing is sent: the app never switches to another one.
