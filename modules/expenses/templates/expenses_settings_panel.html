Expense Category VAT Defaults

JSON map of category → default VAT rate (%) and deductible (%). Applied when picking a category on the expense form (never overwrites a field you already touched).

Deduction checks

Home supplies are deductible at 30 % of this share (LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª b). Leave empty if you do not work from home; expenses are then only checked against the 30 % maximum. The checks only show notices — they never change an expense.
