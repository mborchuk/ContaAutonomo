VAT breakdown (IVA soportado)
Check the VAT deduction. In Spain the VAT of an expense can normally be deducted only with an invoice issued to you with your NIF (Ley 37/1992 del IVA, art. 97; Reglamento de facturación, RD 1619/2012, arts. 6 and 7); a ticket without it does not qualify. Ask the supplier for a full invoice with your NIF. If the VAT cannot be deducted, set the VAT rate to 0: the net becomes the whole amount, so all of it counts as the expense and no VAT is deducted. Check with your gestor.
For invoices without Spanish VAT that say "reverse charge". Leave VAT at 0: the VAT is declared and deducted on Modelo 303.
