Yearly VAT return (from 2027)

Real Decreto-ley 43/2026 adds a voluntary yearly VAT return for individuals whose volume of operations in the previous year did not exceed 85,000 EUR. One return for the year, filed in January, instead of four quarterly Modelo 303. The AEAT debits three advances on 20 April, 20 July and 20 October, each a quarter of the previous year's result. You opt in by declaración censal: for 2027 in March 2027, then in December for the next year. Renouncing binds for three years. It cannot be combined with the régimen simplificado, the monthly refund register or cash accounting (criterio de caja).
